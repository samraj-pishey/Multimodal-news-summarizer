Article · video · speech

Long story. Short summary.

A trained model reads a news article alongside its video, then picks the key sentences and key frames. It runs entirely in your browser.

Upload an article, a video, or both. Both gives the most complete result; a video alone is summarized from its speech and is the least reliable. Your files stay on this device. The first run downloads up to about 130 MB of models. Scanned PDFs won't work, and only the first 10 minutes of video are used.
